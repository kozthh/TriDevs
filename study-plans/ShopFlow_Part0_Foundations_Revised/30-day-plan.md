# ShopFlow Part 0 - Foundations: 30-Day Study Tracker

## How to use this tracker
- Study 2 to 3 hours per day.
- Use 6 days for active study and 1 day for retrospective.
- Keep one running notes file with definitions, questions, and summaries.
- Try to produce one tangible output every day, even if it is small.

## Week 1: Document map and first-pass understanding

Day 1:
- Read the PDF title, headings, and any section labels.
- Write a one-page outline of the document structure.
- List the top 10 unfamiliar terms or ideas.

Day 2:
- Read the opening section slowly.
- Write the central purpose of the PDF in one paragraph.
- Record what problem the document seems to solve.

Day 3:
- Read the next section and highlight repeated ideas.
- Turn each repeated idea into a short definition.
- Add one example of how the idea might show up in practice.

Day 4:
- Re-read the first major concept section.
- Convert the prose into bullet points.
- Write down any assumptions the PDF makes.

Day 5:
- Review the notes from the first four days.
- Build a glossary of important terms.
- Mark any concepts that still feel vague.

Day 6:
- Explain the document structure from memory.
- Rebuild the outline without looking at the PDF.
- Note which sections need a deeper second pass.

Day 7:
- Review what you learned.
- Note the strongest points and the weakest points.
- Decide what to reread next week.

## Week 2: Core concepts and vocabulary

Day 8:
- Re-open the glossary and outline.
- Read the next major section.
- Write the section goal in plain language.

Day 9:
- Break the section into smaller ideas.
- Write one sentence for each idea.
- Add a question beside each idea if something is unclear.

Day 10:
- Focus on examples, diagrams, or walkthroughs.
- Reconstruct each example from memory.
- Compare your version to the PDF.

Day 11:
- Re-read the same section with a focus on precision.
- Replace vague wording in your notes with accurate wording.
- Add any missing definitions.

Day 12:
- Create a summary sheet for the week.
- Include definitions, relationships, and any simple flow diagrams.
- Keep the sheet short enough to review quickly.

Day 13:
- Test recall without the PDF.
- Say or write the main ideas from memory.
- Mark the pieces you could not explain confidently.

Day 14:
- Review the week.
- Clean up your notes.
- Decide what should be repeated later.

## Week 3: Application and mental models

Day 15:
- Review the previous summary sheets.
- Identify the strongest mental model so far.
- Write it as a flow, chain, or sequence.

Day 16:
- Read the next section through the lens of cause and effect.
- Note what depends on what.
- Add arrows or numbered steps to your notes.

Day 17:
- Focus on constraints, tradeoffs, or exceptions.
- Write the advantage and downside of each.
- Capture any warnings the document gives.

Day 18:
- Revisit the examples.
- Reproduce the logic without checking the page first.
- Compare, then correct.

Day 19:
- Build a one-page review note for the whole PDF so far.
- Separate core ideas from supporting details.
- Keep the most reusable ideas at the top.

Day 20:
- Do a closed-book recap.
- Write a short explanation of the PDF as if teaching someone else.
- List the concepts that still need work.

Day 21:
- Retrospective day.
- Review the week’s notes.
- Identify the top 3 topics to revisit.

## Week 4: Consolidation and final recall

Day 22:
- Read only your summaries, not the full PDF.
- Spot any missing gaps.
- Add short corrections where needed.

Day 23:
- Create a final glossary for the document.
- Make each definition short and exact.
- Remove duplicates.

Day 24:
- Write a final one-page summary of the PDF.
- Include purpose, structure, and main lessons.
- Keep it easy to scan.

Day 25:
- Rebuild the document outline from memory.
- Compare it to the actual structure.
- Fix any missing branches or sections.

Day 26:
- Re-read the weakest pages or sections.
- Rewrite those notes in simpler language.
- Make them easier to revisit later.

Day 27:
- Create a final revision sheet.
- Put the most important ideas, terms, and reminders on one page.
- Add one action item for future practice.

Day 28:
- Do a final closed-book self-test.
- Explain the PDF from memory.
- Check whether you can do it cleanly and calmly.

Day 29:
- Review the self-test results.
- Correct the mistakes in your notes.
- Write down the last unclear topic.

Day 30:
- Final retrospective.
- Write what you learned from the PDF overall.
- Decide how you will review this material again later.
