# ShopFlow Part E - Career Interview Readiness: 30-Day Study Tracker

## How to use this tracker
- Study 2 to 3 hours per day.
- Use 6 days for active study and 1 day for retrospective.
- Keep notes on resume, project stories, behavioral answers, technical explanations, and mock interviews.
- End each day with one polished artifact or one practice response.

## Week 1: Career readiness orientation

Day 1:
- Read the headings and map the document structure.
- Identify the main career-readiness themes.
- Write a rough outline of the curriculum.

Day 2:
- Read the opening sections carefully.
- Write what the curriculum expects you to prepare.
- Note the roles, outcomes, or hiring stages mentioned.

Day 3:
- Capture the main interview concepts as short definitions.
- Add unfamiliar terms to a glossary.
- Write a plain-language explanation for each.

Day 4:
- Review any examples, templates, or sample answers.
- Explain what each example is showing.
- Note the structure of a strong response.

Day 5:
- Build a glossary grouped by resume, portfolio, behavior, technical interview, networking, or follow-up.
- Mark the concepts that keep appearing.
- Add one example for each important term.

Day 6:
- Recreate the document structure from memory.
- Give a short explanation of the curriculum’s purpose.
- List the sections that need a slower second pass.

Day 7:
- Retrospective day.
- Write what feels clear and what still feels abstract.
- Pick the next section to revisit.

## Week 2: Resume, portfolio, and narrative

Day 8:
- Revisit your notes.
- Read the resume and portfolio sections.
- Identify the preferred framing.

Day 9:
- Focus on resume structure.
- Write the sections that belong on a strong resume.
- Capture advice about clarity, brevity, or impact.

Day 10:
- Study bullet writing and achievement framing.
- Rewrite weak bullets into stronger action-result statements.
- Note any metrics or evidence patterns.

Day 11:
- Review portfolio or project presentation guidance.
- Write how to explain one project in under two minutes.
- Capture the proof points a reviewer should notice.

Day 12:
- Compare different ways to describe the same experience.
- Note what makes one version stronger.
- Summarize the personal narrative lesson in a paragraph.

Day 13:
- Rebuild a resume checklist from memory.
- Recite your project story without looking at notes.
- Identify weak spots.

Day 14:
- Retrospective day.
- Review resume and narrative notes.
- Decide what needs more polishing.

## Week 3: Interview performance and practice

Day 15:
- Re-open the interview and practice sections.
- Identify the behavioral and technical themes.
- List the terms that connect to interview performance.

Day 16:
- Study behavioral interview guidance carefully.
- Write the practical meaning of each recommendation.
- Turn each into a checklist item.

Day 17:
- Focus on technical interview structure.
- Note how the PDF expects you to explain reasoning.
- Summarize the expectations in your own words.

Day 18:
- Review mock interview or rehearsal advice.
- Write the patterns that make practice sessions useful.
- Mark any anti-patterns or warning signs.

Day 19:
- Look for communication, confidence, or problem-solving guidance.
- Summarize what helps answers feel clear and credible.
- Add any cautions or tradeoffs.

Day 20:
- Run a closed-book recap of the interview topics.
- Rebuild a mock-interview checklist from memory.
- Write one paragraph explaining why the rules matter.

Day 21:
- Retrospective day.
- Pick the most actionable interview skills.
- Decide which checklist item should become default.

## Week 4: Consolidation and readiness

Day 22:
- Review all prior notes.
- Identify the top career-readiness ideas to retain.
- Mark anything still confusing.

Day 23:
- Re-read the weakest sections only.
- Rewrite them in simpler language.
- Reduce them to short, reusable rules.

Day 24:
- Create a final interview prep sheet.
- Include resume, portfolio, behavioral, and technical notes.
- Keep the sheet easy to scan.

Day 25:
- Run one full mock interview using the PDF guidance.
- Write the answer structure and evidence you used.
- Note what the mock session taught you.

Day 26:
- Take a closed-book review of the entire PDF.
- Compare your memory to the source.
- Fix the errors in your notes.

Day 27:
- Write the final summary for this PDF.
- Include the most important interview lessons.
- Add one practice item for future work.

Day 28:
- Run a final closed-book self-test.
- Explain the PDF from memory.
- Mark the remaining weak spots.

Day 29:
- Review the self-test results.
- Fix mistakes in your notes.
- Write the last unresolved question.

Day 30:
- Final retrospective.
- Summarize what you learned overall.
- Decide how you will revisit the material later.
